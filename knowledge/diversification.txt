Portfolio Diversification

Diversification involves spreading investments across different assets,
sectors, securities, or other categories rather than depending heavily on
one investment.

A portfolio with substantial exposure to a single security, sector, or asset
class may have concentration risk.

When evaluating a portfolio, concentration should be considered together
with the investor's risk profile and investment objectives.

Diversification can reduce exposure to the poor performance of an individual
holding or category, but diversification does not eliminate investment risk.
