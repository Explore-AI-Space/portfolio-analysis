Investor Risk Profiles

Conservative investors generally have a lower tolerance for investment losses
and portfolio volatility. Advisory recommendations should place greater
emphasis on capital preservation, liquidity, and controlling portfolio risk.

Moderate investors generally seek a balance between long-term growth and
risk management. Recommendations should consider diversification, controlled
equity exposure, and the investor's investment horizon.

Aggressive investors generally have a higher tolerance for market volatility
and investment losses and may accept greater portfolio risk in pursuit of
long-term growth.

Risk tolerance should not be considered in isolation. Portfolio
recommendations should also consider the investor's financial goals,
investment horizon, liquidity requirements, and current holdings.
