
Portfolio Rebalancing

Rebalancing is the process of adjusting portfolio holdings when the portfolio
has moved away from its intended allocation.

An advisory recommendation to rebalance should consider the investor's risk
profile, investment objectives, investment horizon, current portfolio
allocation, and the reason for the deviation.

Rebalancing recommendations should explain which portfolio characteristics
are driving the recommendation and should communicate relevant risks and
trade-offs.

Short-term market movements alone should not automatically be treated as
sufficient justification for a portfolio change.
