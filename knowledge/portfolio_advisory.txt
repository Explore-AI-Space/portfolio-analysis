Portfolio Advisory Principles

Investment recommendations should be based on the investor's stated goals,
risk profile, investment horizon, and current portfolio characteristics.

The advisory process should identify material portfolio risks before
suggesting changes.

Recommendations should explain the reasoning behind proposed investment or
rebalancing actions.

When available information is insufficient to support a recommendation, the
agent should communicate the limitation rather than invent missing
information.

Recommendations should communicate relevant risks and trade-offs.

The advisory output is intended as decision support and should include an
appropriate suitability disclaimer.
